# Product Truth: FAMILY Private Wealth Platform

## 1. Vision & Purpose
**FAMILY** is a sovereign, multi-tenant private wealth control and family treasury platform. It empowers individuals and family offices (50+ independent tenants) to track, reconcile, and allocate their liquid net worth, banking accounts, investment holdings, and recurring cash flow with double-entry accounting rigor and absolute privacy.

## 2. Audience & Operating Context
- **Target Audience:** Family heads, high-net-worth individuals, private clients, and wealth advisors.
- **Operating Context:**
  - **Mobile (PWA, 390×844px+):** Instant 3-second daily expense logging, OCR receipt capture (Instapay / E-Wallets), fast balance checking with thumb-friendly controls.
  - **Desktop (1440×900px+):** Executive balance sheet, dual-mode analysis (Simple Family Mode vs. Pro Advisor Mode), double-entry ledger audits, risk rebalancing, and tax/zakat compliance.

## 3. Brand Voice & Emotional Resonance
- **Tone:** Sovereign, calm, prestigious, trustworthy, and bank-grade.
- **Visual Stance:** Restrained luxury. Deep, high-contrast surfaces without neon artifacts or fuzzy AI gradients.
- **Anti-Posture:** No playful cartoons, no crypto hype, no generic "AI Slop" (no purple glows, no nested card labyrinths, no washed-out gray text on colored buttons).

## 4. Architectural & Design Directives
- **Zero AI Slop:** Strictly enforce Impeccable deterministic detector rules (0 findings on `impeccable detect`).
- **Touch Ergonomics:** Every interactive element on mobile must meet or exceed `48px` height with thumb-accessible positioning.
- **Financial Typography:** Strict tabular numbers (`tabular-nums`) with clear currency indicators (`EGP`, `USD`, `SAR`) and hierarchy.
- **Data Isolation:** 100% strict user data isolation across workspaces and accounts.
